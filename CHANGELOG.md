# Changelog

## 1.0.5 (Minecraft 1.21.1, NeoForge)

### Fixed
- Big Bertha, Slice, Royal Guardian Sword and Attitude Adjuster no longer shoot little Big Bertha pictures when you swing them. The swing hit is invisible again, like in the original OreSpawn.
- Attitude Adjuster swings now work like the original:
  - Range, knockback and launch height use the original values.
  - Hitting a mob causes a small explosion where the swing lands.
  - Swinging at a block now causes a bigger explosion. Before this, nothing happened.
  - Explosions can start fires, and break blocks only when the `mobGriefing` game rule is on.
- The big weapons no longer kill your Girlfriend or Boyfriend.
- The big weapons' swing travels at the original speed again, aims where your head is facing, and measures its reach the same way as the original.
